Araw-Araw na Gabay

Isang 365-day Filipino devotional application designed around Scripture, daily reflection, prayer, practical action, and personal reflection.

Bible translation: King James Version, first published in 1611. The KJV is a historic English translation. It is not the original first-century manuscripts.

KJV text is retrieved from the DailyBible.ca KJV API and cached on the device after it is viewed. Reference selection follows a curated 365-day KJV verse archive, with Filipino applications written for everyday life.
